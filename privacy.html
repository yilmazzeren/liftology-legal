Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Welcome to LiftologyAI. Your privacy is paramount to us. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect, how it is used, and how your data is protected when using our iOS application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: If you sign up or log in via Apple Sign-In or Email, we store your email address and an encrypted user ID.
  • Fitness & Workout Data: Sets, reps, weight logs, exercise selections, routine templates, and physical metrics (height, weight, age) that you input to personalize workout progression.
  • Device & Diagnostics: Anonymous diagnostic logs and crash reports to improve application stability and performance.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide AI-powered coaching, progressive overload recommendations, and workout analytics.
  • To sync your workout logs securely across devices via Supabase.
  • To manage AI tokens, subscriptions, or credit purchases securely via RevenueCat and Apple In-App Purchases.

3. Third-Party Services

We work with trusted industry-standard infrastructure providers:

  • Apple: For authentication (Sign in with Apple) and In-App Purchases.
  • Supabase: For secure database storage and authentication.
  • RevenueCat: For handling in-app purchase receipts and entitlement status.
  • Anthropic / AI Providers: To generate AI workout suggestions without sharing personally identifiable information.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You have full control over your data. You may delete your account and all associated workout logs at any time directly within the application under Profile > Delete Account. Upon confirmation, your data is permanently removed from our databases.

5. Security

All communication between the app and our backend uses industry-standard TLS encryption. We do not sell your personal or fitness data to any third parties.
